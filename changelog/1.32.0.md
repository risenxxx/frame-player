### New

- **The mouse wheel can seek instead of changing the volume.** A new setting under Playback makes the wheel seek, with Shift and the wheel then changing the volume, and a second one sets how far each notch of the wheel seeks: 1, 5, 10 or 30 seconds. Volume stays the default. While casting, the wheel is still the television's volume.
- **The player speaks Simplified Chinese.** Choose 中文 under Settings → General, or let the player pick it up from a Chinese system. Every menu, setting and message is translated, including the macOS menu bar, and the catalog then shows titles and descriptions in Chinese. A system set to Traditional Chinese still starts in English.

### Improvements and fixes

- **The HDR setting explains itself on a Mac.** Its hint used to say that Windows HDR has to be on; on a Mac it now says what HDR output actually needs there, brightness headroom on the display.
- **Subtitle search finds Chinese, Portuguese and Azerbaijani subtitles.** Searching in any of these three languages used to come back empty, because the subtitle site names each of them by region or script. Chinese now finds both Simplified and Traditional subtitles.
