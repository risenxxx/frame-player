### Improvements and fixes

- **Cursor hides reliably on macOS.** Moving the pointer past the window buttons in the top-left corner no longer leaves the cursor visible after the controls have hidden.
- **Interrupted seekbar drags.** A drag on the seekbar that the system cancels no longer leaves the video paused with controls that never fade.
