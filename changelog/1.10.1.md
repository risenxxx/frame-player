### Improvements and fixes

- **Playback options.** The button that holds the folded controls is now a gear with the tooltip "Playback options" instead of three dots.
- **Back row in folded panels.** When the bar is folded, the queue, chapter, track and TV panels start with a "Back" row that returns to the playback options.
