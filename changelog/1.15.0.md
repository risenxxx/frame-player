### New

- **Crop black bars.** The context menu has a Crop option that removes black bars baked into the picture. Auto looks for bars in frames from across the whole file; you can also pick 2.39, 1.85 or 4:3. The choice is remembered for each file, and hover previews on the seekbar are cropped to match. Auto works only for files on disk.
- **Adjustable seek steps.** The short and long seek steps are no longer fixed at 5 and 10 seconds. Settings lets you pick the short step from 1 to 10 seconds and the long one from 10 to 60, and the hotkey labels and menus show the values you chose.
