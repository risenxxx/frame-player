### Improvements and fixes

- **More DLNA TVs.** The player now understands the different ways TVs name the same video formats and describe themselves, so files are no longer refused by TVs that spell them differently.
- **Router port check.** Checking whether the router opened the torrent port works with more routers.
- **Clearing history clears everything.** Clearing the watch history, or excluding a folder, now also removes remembered video titles, remembered track choices and the list of downloaded subtitles, which were left behind before.
