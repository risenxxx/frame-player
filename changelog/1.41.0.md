### New

- **Volume leveling is one key away.** Volume leveling now has a shortcut, N, which you can change in the shortcut settings, and a switch in the Playback section of the right-click menu, so you no longer have to open the audio settings to turn it on late at night. While it is on, a small icon next to the volume slider shows it, and clicking the icon turns leveling off.
