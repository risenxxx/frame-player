### Improvements and fixes

- **Room chip opens the room.** The room indicator in the top bar is now a button that shows who is in the room and its rules. It stays visible while the pointer rests on it.
- **Clearer member list.** The room panel shows how many people are in the room and lists them as separate rows.
- **Copy confirmation.** "Copied" now appears on the button you pressed, the code or the link, rather than always on the code.
- **Better invitation page.** The web page a room link opens now appears in the visitor's language and offers the right installer for their system when they do not have the player yet.
