### New

- **The window keeps the video's shape.** With "Match video aspect ratio" on, the window no longer just fits the video once when a file opens: dragging an edge keeps the picture's proportions, and the window cannot be made smaller than a sensible size for that shape. The setting is now on by default.

### Improvements and fixes

- **Rotated videos.** A video that is rotated, such as a phone clip shot upright, now gets an upright window instead of a wide one with the picture in the middle.
