### Improvements and fixes

- **Correct colors in HDR previews.** Seekbar previews of HDR10 and HLG videos are now converted properly and no longer look washed out or off in hue. Ordinary videos are slightly more accurate too.
- **Dolby Vision previews are no longer pink.** Previews of Dolby Vision profile 5 videos now show close to the real colors. This affects only the previews; playback and saved frames were already correct.
- **Leaving a torrent that has not started.** Going back to the start screen while a torrent is still looking for peers no longer leaves the loading message on screen.
