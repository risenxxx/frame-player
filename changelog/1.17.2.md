### Improvements and fixes

- **A fade to black between files.** When the next file starts, the picture goes dark first and returns once the window has taken the new video's shape, instead of briefly showing black bars before the window moves.
- **Steadier resizing.** While you drag a window edge, the opposite corner stays in place, and the controls and other overlays step aside until the window stops moving, so they no longer appear doubled or out of place during the resize.
