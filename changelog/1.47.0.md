### New

- **Two subtitles at once.** The subtitle menu has a "Second subtitles" row: pick another track and it is shown at the top of the picture, in the same look as the first and stepping down below the title bar while the controls are up — handy for learning a language. It can come from a file you add or from the subtitle search, has its own delay, is remembered for the folder like the first one, and a shortcut (Alt+V, Option+V on a Mac) hides it for a while without forgetting it. The subtitle button reads both languages, for example RU+EN.

### Improvements and fixes

- **A button's tooltip no longer blinks back after a click.** Clicking a button whose icon changes, such as mute or play, hid its tooltip and then brought it back a moment later although the mouse had not moved. It now stays hidden until the pointer leaves the button.
