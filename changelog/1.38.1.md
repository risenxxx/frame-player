### Improvements and fixes

- **On Windows, a double click on the top bar now goes full screen.** While a video is open, a double click on the bar along the top of the window does the same as a double click on the picture: it enters full screen, and the next double click anywhere brings the window back. Before, it maximized the window, which looks almost the same, and double clicks on the picture then switched between full screen and maximized without ever returning to the normal window. A window maximized another way is restored by the double click, as before.
