### Improvements and fixes

- **Dub choice carries across a torrent season.** The audio track and subtitles you pick for one episode of a torrent are now used for the next episodes too. Before, each episode started over, and on releases with several dubs in one language the wrong one was often chosen.
- **Forgetting a torrent forgets its track choice.** Removing a torrent from the history also removes the dub remembered for it.
