### Improvements and fixes

- **Pointer cursor on controls.** Every button and slider now shows the hand cursor, including a few that looked inactive before.
- **Readable seekbar time.** The time and chapter name shown while hovering the seekbar have a stronger shadow and weight, so they stay legible over bright frames.
