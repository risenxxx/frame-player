### New

- **An audio offset for each speaker and pair of headphones.** Bluetooth speakers and headphones play sound a little late, and the picture runs ahead of it. The audio menu now has a second offset under the file's delay, kept for the device the sound is going to: set it once for your speaker and it applies to every video while that speaker is connected, and goes away when the sound moves back to the built-in speakers.
- **See how well everybody in a room is keeping up.** A dot beside each name in a watch-together room says whether that person is in step: green when all is well, yellow or red when their connection is slow or uneven, or when their playback keeps slipping behind because the source cannot keep up. Hover it to see which, and on your own row the ping to the room server.
