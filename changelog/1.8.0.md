### New

- **RSS feeds for torrents.** Paste a tracker's RSS feed for a release into the link box instead of a magnet link. If the feed holds several torrents, you choose one; the player remembers the feed.
- **New episodes are noticed.** For a torrent opened from a feed, the start screen's torrent list marks it with RSS and shows "Newer upload out" when the tracker has published a re-upload with more episodes. Updating keeps the episodes already downloaded and your watch history.
- **Feeds for existing torrents.** The update dialog also accepts a feed, so a torrent added by magnet can be switched to following its RSS feed from then on.
