### Improvements and fixes

- **Loading shows how long it has been.** While a magnet link is being resolved, or while the room's film is being opened, the loading panel shows the seconds elapsed, so a slow start does not look like a frozen player. The hint now says that finding the file list can take up to a minute and a half.
- **Watch together says why.** When the player cannot open what the room is watching, the room panel now gives the reason. A member whose torrent never actually starts playing is no longer counted as ready, so the room does not run ahead of them.
- **Deleting a torrent always finishes.** Deleting a torrent right after leaving it, or one still resolving, could leave the delete button doing nothing. It now completes, and if something does go wrong it says what.
- **Rejected mpv.conf is reported.** If mpv refuses a setting in your mpv.conf, the whole file is skipped. The player now shows a popup at startup and a note in Settings with mpv's own message.
- **Settings tabs stay in view.** The title and tab row stay pinned while the settings scroll, switching tabs starts at the top, and the current tab is always scrolled into view.
