### Improvements and fixes

- **The Picture menu is split in two.** Right-clicking the video now shows Geometry — rotation, aspect ratio, crop and fill the screen — apart from Picture, which keeps sharpening and the brightness and color sliders. Each panel is shorter, and the picture panel's shortcut now opens straight onto the sliders.
