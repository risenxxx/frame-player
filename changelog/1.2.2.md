### Improvements and fixes

- **Casting controls follow the TV.** While a video plays on the TV, the chapter list, the skip button and jumps with the number keys now use the TV's position instead of the moment casting began.
- **No stale lists.** Track, chapter and queue lists, the recent videos and the torrent readout could occasionally show results for a file that was no longer open. The newest answer now always wins.
