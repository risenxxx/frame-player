### Improvements and fixes

- **Member status in a room.** Each member can now show "opening", "failed" or "no copy", so everyone can see who is holding the room up and why. If opening the room's film fails, a "Try again" button appears.
- **Guests find the torrent faster.** A torrent shared through a room now carries its trackers, so guests on networks that block the DHT can still find peers.
- **Why a torrent is not connecting.** When no peers answer, or peers are found but no connection is made, the loading panel now says that a VPN or an internet provider blocking BitTorrent is the usual cause.
- **Accurate error for torrent streams.** When a torrent file cannot be read because its data never arrived, the error now says so instead of suggesting yt-dlp is out of date.
