### New

- **Watch on TV.** Send the playing video to a Chromecast or a DLNA television on your network. While it plays there, the window works as a remote: play, pause, seeking, volume and the queue all control the TV, and disconnecting continues playback here from the same moment.
- **Two ways to reach a TV.** The player picks the best route automatically. Over DLNA many TVs play the file exactly as it is, with every track; for Chromecast a file the TV cannot open is prepared first or streamed with stereo sound. Each device's settings let you choose the route yourself.
- **Torrents on the TV.** A torrent that is still downloading can be sent to a DLNA TV once enough of it has arrived ahead of playback.
- **Device check.** If casting does not work, a check tests the network and the device step by step and gives you a report you can copy. It does not play anything.
- **Prepared copies cache.** Files prepared for a TV can be kept so the next cast starts instantly, with a size limit and a button to clear them in the new TV settings tab.

### Improvements and fixes

- **Posters for torrent episodes.** Episodes watched from a torrent now get a poster on the start screen while they are still downloading.
- **Folder queues on Windows.** The queue is built correctly even when paths differ only in letter case or slash direction.
