### Improvements and fixes

- **A shared room no longer speeds itself up.** While watching together, a pause or a seek pressed at the wrong moment could make the whole room play slightly fast — 1.04×, then 1.14× — without anybody having changed the speed. The room now keeps the speed somebody actually chose.
- **Leaving for the start screen cancels a torrent that is still opening.** Going back to the start screen while a torrent was still being found could leave the loading note standing there, or let the film start by itself a moment later. Leaving now drops everything that was on its way.
- **A torrent opened from the catalog no longer waits in a black window.** Opening a release from the catalog, or a torrent from "continue watching", could leave the window black — no controls, no loading note — until the first frame arrived. The loading note and the controls now stay up for the whole wait.
