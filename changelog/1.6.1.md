### New

- **Release filters.** In the catalog you can now set a minimum quality, hide releases nobody is seeding, and sort by size as well as by quality or seeds. If the filters hide everything, the panel says how many releases they hid.
- **Automatic torrent updates.** For a season opened from the catalog, "Update torrent" now looks for the newer upload of the same release by itself. For a torrent opened any other way, it still asks you to paste the new link.
