### New

- **Plays almost anything.** Decoding comes from mpv and FFmpeg, with hardware acceleration, 10-bit video, HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision profiles 5 and 8.
- **Local files, links and torrents in one window.** Open files, paste a link to a stream or a page yt-dlp understands, or paste a magnet link and start watching while it downloads. Seeding is off by default.
- **Seekbar previews.** Hovering the seekbar shows thumbnails, and when the cursor stops the preview sharpens to the exact frame you would land on.
- **Chapters and a skip button.** A chapter list, plus a button to skip openings, recaps, previews, credits and ads.
- **Subtitle search.** Find subtitles on OpenSubtitles by the file's own fingerprint, with a title search as the fallback.
- **Resume anywhere.** Files, links and torrent episodes reopen where you stopped, from a start screen of unfinished videos. Your choice of audio and subtitle track carries over to the next episode.
- **Playback tools.** Frame stepping in both directions, an A–B loop, repeat modes, playback speed, zoom and pan, frame export, and a live media info panel.
- **A queue from the folder.** Opening one file queues the rest of its folder in natural order.
- **Mini player.** A small always-on-top window that snaps to the screen corners and, on macOS, floats over other apps in fullscreen.
- **Yours to configure.** Every hotkey can be rebound and works in any keyboard layout, the settings dialog writes to your own mpv.conf, and folders can be kept out of the watch history.
- **Automatic updates.** The player offers signed updates and reopens the current video at the same position afterwards. The interface is in English and Russian.
