### New

- **A setting for what a click on the picture does.** Settings → General. The default is what it was: a click pauses, a double click goes fullscreen. "Fullscreen only" leaves pausing to Space and the controls, so a double click can never land as a pause and nothing blinks under it; "Pause only" leaves fullscreen to its key and its button.

### Improvements and fixes

- **Clicking an inactive window on macOS no longer pauses the video.** The click that brings the player forward is not a command any more: the video keeps playing, and a double click on an inactive window goes straight to fullscreen instead of landing as a pause. The window also takes that first press for what it is, so it can be dragged by the bar or the picture — and the mini player moved — without clicking it once first.
- **Taking AirPods out pauses the video on macOS.** The system's own playback commands reach the player now: AirPods taken out of the ears pause it and put back in resume it, and the keyboard's play/pause key and the Now Playing controls in Control Center work too.
