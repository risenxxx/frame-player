### New

- **Play several audio tracks at once.** The audio menu has a "Several tracks" switch: with it on, ticking tracks makes them play together, each at its own level — for a recording that keeps the game, the microphone and a call in separate tracks. The next file opens on one track as usual.
- **External dubs and subtitles in a release's subfolders load by themselves.** Audio tracks shipped beside the video are now picked up the way subtitles already were, and both are found in nested folders such as one per studio or subtitle group. Each one is named after its folder in the track menu, and the choice is remembered for the rest of the season.
- **Choose whether the seekbar preview switches to the exact frame.** When the cursor rests over the seekbar, the preview changes to the frame a click would land on. That can now be turned off in the settings to keep the nearest storyboard frame instead.

### Improvements and fixes

- **Settings are grouped into sections.** Each tab now groups its options into labelled blocks, the window options from the context menu are also in the settings, and the version and licenses moved to an About section.
