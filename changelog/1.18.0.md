### New

- **Lists fade at their edges.** The queue, chapters, audio and subtitle tracks, the cast picker and the overflow menu now fade out at the top and bottom when there is more to scroll. The item that is playing stays clear of the fade.

### Improvements and fixes

- **Menus keep their width.** On macOS a scrolling menu no longer opens slightly too narrow and then widens the first time you hover over it.
- **Picture adjustments.** The reset button in the picture panel is now the same size as the other buttons in that sheet.
