### Improvements and fixes

- **The volume bar no longer drops to zero while you seek with the wheel.** Seeking with the wheel or a sideways swipe silences the sound for the moment it takes, and the volume bar and the mute icon used to show that as the volume going to zero. They now stay where you set them.
