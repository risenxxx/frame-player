### Improvements and fixes

- **"Size" opens the window pixel for pixel on Windows.** With display scaling above 100 %, the "Size" choice and the 50/100/200 % window sizes made the window larger than the video by the scaling factor: at 175 %, a 1280×688 film opened in a window about 2240 pixels wide. On Windows they now count screen pixels, so the film opens at exactly 1280×688, as it does in mpv, and the picture is shown without being rescaled. On macOS nothing changes.
