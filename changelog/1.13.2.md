### Improvements and fixes

- **Faster launch.** With "remember size and position" on, the window could take about three seconds to appear. It now shows as soon as it is ready.
- **Mini player snaps on release.** The mini player snaps to a screen edge only once you let go of the mouse, not when you pause mid-drag.
- **Seekbar preview in small windows.** The hover preview shrinks to fit a small mini player, and shows only the time when there is no room, instead of running off the top of the window.
- **Seekbar alignment.** The seekbar and timestamps now line up with the same edge as the other controls.
