### New

- **Accept incoming connections.** A new torrent setting asks your router to open a port for the player (UPnP), so peers that could not reach you before can connect. It is off by default, and the setting shows whether the router actually opened the port.

### Improvements and fixes

- **Subtitles stay readable.** While the control bar is on screen, subtitles move up above it instead of hiding behind the seekbar.
