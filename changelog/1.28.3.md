### Improvements and fixes

- **One slow device on the network no longer hides every DLNA television.** When something on the network announced itself and then did not answer, such as a NAS reachable at only one of its addresses, the cast picker could keep saying it was still searching and never list a television that had answered at once. Each television now appears as soon as it has answered, whatever the others do.
