### Improvements and fixes

- **Opening from "Continue watching" no longer blinks.** The controls and the "Resuming" note used to appear, vanish at once while the window took the video's shape, and fade back in. They now come in once, after the window has settled.
- **Button labels and the app name no longer break onto two lines.** Right after launch, "Open file (O)" on the start screen and the name in the title bar's corner could wrap onto a second line in a window with plenty of room. The edge of the Catalog button's outline is also as thick on the right as on the left again.
