### New

- **Watch together.** Choose "Watch together…" in the context menu to create a room, then send its code or link to the others. Position, pause and speed are shared; volume, tracks and the window stay your own. Only the position and pause travel through the room server, never the video.
- **Invitation links.** A room link opens Frame Player and joins the room directly, on both Windows and macOS.
- **Room rules.** The host can let only themselves control playback, and can choose whether the audio track and the subtitles are shared. A shared track is matched by its description, so it works even when people have different rips.
- **Nobody is left behind.** The room pauses while anyone is still loading and shows who it is waiting for. Small drift between viewers is corrected by briefly adjusting speed rather than jumping.
- **Checking you have the same file.** When you open your own copy of what the room is watching, the room tells you whether it is the same release, a different rip whose timing may drift a little, or a different file. A file in a hidden folder never has its name sent to the room.
- **Room server setting.** Settings → General has an address for the room server, for anyone who runs their own.
