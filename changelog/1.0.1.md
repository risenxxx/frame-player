### Improvements and fixes

- **Deleted torrents stay deleted.** A torrent removed from the list could reappear as an empty copy after the player was restarted. It is now removed for good.
- **Torrent names.** Downloaded torrents in the list show the release's own name, and any torrent on disk can be opened again from there.
- **Smoother scroll fade.** The fade at the bottom of scrolling lists no longer flickers when the list changes.
