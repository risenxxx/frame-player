### New

- **Choose where torrents are saved.** Settings now has a folder for downloaded torrents. By default they go to the app's cache folder, which the system may empty. Changing it moves nothing: torrents already downloaded stay where they are and keep opening.
- **Delete watched episodes.** Deleting a torrent from the start screen now asks what to remove: only the episodes you have watched to the end, or everything. The rest of the season stays and plays immediately.
- **Watched episodes are marked.** In a torrent's file list, episodes you have finished show a tick and a dimmer name, so a season of similar names is easy to navigate.

### Improvements and fixes

- **Safer deletion in your own folder.** When you have chosen a folder, deleting torrent data removes only what the player downloaded there.
- **Clearing history is complete.** "Clear history" in Settings now also removes the remembered links of torrents you have opened.
