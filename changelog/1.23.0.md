### New

- **The end screen can be put away to see the last frame.** A Hide button under the cards, or Escape, uncovers the final frame of the video and stops the countdown to the next one. The cards come back at the next ending.
- **Choose how soon the controls hide.** A new setting decides how long the mouse has to rest before the controls and the cursor go away, from the moment it stops to five seconds.

### Improvements and fixes

- **End-of-video cards keep their edges over a black frame.** They used to dissolve into a dark final frame, especially when there was no picture to show for them; they now carry an outline and a shadow that read on dark and bright frames alike.
