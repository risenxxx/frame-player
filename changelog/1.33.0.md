### New

- **Play on an Apple TV or an AirPlay television from a Mac.** The cast menu on macOS has an AirPlay row that opens the system's own list of AirPlay receivers. Pick a television and it plays the file itself while the player becomes its remote: pause, play and seeking work from the window, and disconnecting carries on in the player from the same moment. Files the television cannot take as they are are repacked first, without re-encoding the picture. If nothing starts, the player says so and suggests checking that AirPlay is turned on in the television's settings. AirPlay is not available on Windows.

### Improvements and fixes

- **The cast menu shows what kind of device each row is.** Each television, streaming box and speaker in the cast menu now has an icon, taken from what the device itself announces, and so does the device you are casting to. Google Nest speakers and other sound-only Cast devices are now recognised as speakers rather than offered as screens.
- **Fast frame stepping works on torrents that are still downloading.** Stepping back frame by frame is now quick on a torrent before it has finished, for everything that has already arrived — which includes anything you have just watched. Where the earlier part has not downloaded yet, stepping falls back to the slower way, and if a step has to wait for the torrent the player says so instead of appearing to hang.
