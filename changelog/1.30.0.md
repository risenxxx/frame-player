### New

- **The audio and subtitle buttons say what is playing.** A small tag on the audio button shows the language of the track you hear, or how many are mixed together. The subtitle button shows how many subtitle tracks the file has while they are off, and the language once one is on; with no subtitles at all it stays plain. Hovering either button names the track and where it sits among the others, and the tags carry over to the playback panel when the window is too narrow for the buttons.
