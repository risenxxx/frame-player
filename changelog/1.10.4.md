### Improvements and fixes

- **Torrents resume after an update.** A torrent playing when an update was installed used to hang on "opening the link" after the restart and then show an error. It now reopens and continues where you were; if it cannot, the start screen appears instead.
- **Quieter queue.** Rows in the queue and their remove buttons no longer show tooltips.
