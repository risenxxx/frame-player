### Improvements and fixes

- **Seeking with the wheel now works on a mouse.** Scrolling sideways over the picture moves through the film — a gesture a trackpad can make and a mouse cannot. Holding Shift while rolling the wheel is now the same thing: one notch is five seconds, down for forward and up for back.
