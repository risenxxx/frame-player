### Improvements and fixes

- **AirPlay no longer shows a loading spinner over a film that is playing.** With some televisions the casting screen kept its spinner up for the whole time a film played over AirPlay, as if it were still loading. It now goes away as soon as the television is actually playing.
- **The mute button always mutes.** Clicking the sound button could say "Sound: off" and leave the sound playing. It now always switches the sound, and when the sound is sent undecoded to an AV receiver — where the player's volume has no effect — it says that the volume is on the receiver instead.
