### New

- **Move file to Trash.** A new action sends the file that is playing to the Trash on macOS or the Recycle Bin on Windows, where it can be restored. It works only for files on disk, has no key by default, and can be bound in the hotkey settings.
- **SDR video color on macOS.** A new video setting, "As in macOS", draws shadows and midtones lighter, the way QuickTime does. "As in mpv" keeps the previous look. HDR video is not affected.
- **Choose when the controls hide.** The "Hide the controls" setting offers Always, In fullscreen or Never. The mini player counts as fullscreen. A second switch decides whether the cursor hides together with the controls.
