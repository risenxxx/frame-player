### New

- **Torrent route.** A new setting chooses which connection torrents use: Auto (the system decides, which means through the VPN when one is on), Bypass VPN (the computer's own Wi-Fi or cable connection), or a specific network interface. Everything other than torrents stays in the VPN. The settings list the available connections and warn that outside the VPN peers see your real IP address.
- **VPN question.** When traffic goes through a VPN, the first torrent of each run asks whether to download through the VPN or bypass it, with an option to remember the choice. It can be turned off with "Ask about the VPN".
- **No silent fallback.** If the chosen connection is not available, the torrent does not start and the player says so, rather than quietly going through the VPN.
