### New

- **A hue slider in the picture panel.** Next to brightness, contrast, saturation and gamma, hue shifts every colour of the picture around the colour wheel. It is kept per file, per playlist or for all files like the others, and has a pair of shortcuts you can assign in the keyboard settings.
- **The mouse wheel seeks over the seekbar.** With the pointer on the seekbar the wheel now seeks, by the step set for it, even when it controls the volume everywhere else; over the volume slider it always changes the volume. Over the video it still does whichever you chose in the settings.
