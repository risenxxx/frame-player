### New

- **Pinch to resize the window on macOS.** A trackpad pinch now scales the window around its own center, keeping the picture's shape and stopping at the edge of the screen. Zooming into the picture stays on ⌥ + pinch and Ctrl + wheel, and a pinch in fullscreen or in the mini player still zooms. Settings → General has a switch to give the pinch back to the zoom.

### Improvements and fixes

- **Zooming with a pinch follows the fingers.** A pinch used to zoom the picture by a fixed step for every one of the dozens of events a gesture sends, so any pinch ran straight to 800 %. It now zooms by exactly as much as the fingers spread or closed; a mouse wheel still zooms in steps.
