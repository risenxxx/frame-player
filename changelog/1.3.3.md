### Improvements and fixes

- **Readable torrent folders.** Downloaded torrents are now stored in folders named after the torrent instead of a long code. Existing folders are renamed the next time you open that torrent, which costs a one-time check of the downloaded data.
- **Names safe on every system.** Torrent names with characters that are not allowed in file names, or that are very long, are cleaned up so the folder works on both Windows and macOS.
- **Invitation page.** The room invitation page now always offers a download link for visitors who do not have the player, and its text wraps more cleanly.
