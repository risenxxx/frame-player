### Improvements and fixes

- **Faster reopening of torrents.** A torrent you have opened before no longer re-checks all its data every time, which could take seconds on a large season.
- **Torrents stay idle until you ask.** Torrents from the previous session are no longer reconnected to peers when the player starts; nothing is downloaded until you open one.
- **Seeding switch.** Changing the seeding setting could leave the player unable to open any torrent until it was restarted. That is fixed.
- **Stuck tooltips.** A tooltip could stay on screen, sometimes in the top-left corner, after the element under it disappeared, for example when playback started from the start screen.
