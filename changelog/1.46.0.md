### New

- **Pause without bringing up the controls.** A new switch in Settings → General lets the pause key stop the video without putting the control bar over the frame.
- **A thicker seekbar.** Settings → General can make the seekbar thick or thicker, with a bigger knob and a bigger area to grab it by, for mice that are hard to aim with.
- **Shortcuts for subtitles.** V turns subtitles on and off, Shift+V goes to the next subtitle track, and there are actions for the previous track and for larger and smaller subtitles that you can give keys of your own.
- **Volume up to 200%.** With amplification on, the keys and the wheel now raise the volume to 200% instead of 150%, for files that are quiet or for when 150% is not enough. Above the file's own loudness the sound can distort.

### Improvements and fixes

- **The settings close only on a click outside them.** Pressing inside the settings and letting go outside, or the other way round, closed the window. Now it closes only when both happen outside.
- **Escape no longer closes the settings on the shortcuts tab.** On the shortcuts tab Escape cancels a key being recorded, and pressing it once more closed the whole settings window. There it now closes nothing.
- **The subtitle sliders let you see the subtitles.** Dragging a subtitle slider in the settings — size, position, outline or opacity — now makes the settings and the dimming go away until you let go, the way the brightness and colour sliders do, so you can see the result on the picture.
