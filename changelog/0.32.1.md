### New

- **Licenses.** The settings window links to a page listing Frame Player's own license and those of mpv, FFmpeg and the other libraries it uses, with their full texts.

### Improvements and fixes

- **Easier on the battery.** On a laptop running on battery, the seekbar previews are prepared in the background at a much lower processor load. Hovering still shows previews at full speed.
- **Less work while casting.** While a video plays on the TV, the player stops preparing seekbar previews in the background and picks up again when casting ends.
