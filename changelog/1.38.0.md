### New

- **Subtitle files beside the video now reach the television over DLNA.** When you cast over DLNA with a subtitle file chosen in the player (an .srt next to the video, one you added or one you downloaded), the television now gets it along with the film. Picking another subtitle file while the television plays sends the film again from the same moment with the new one. Subtitles inside the file were already listed on the television and are still switched there.

### Improvements and fixes

- **The volume bar follows the mute button.** Muting now takes the volume bar down to zero, and turning the sound back on returns it to where it was. Dragging the bar up, or raising the volume with a key or the wheel, while the sound is off turns it back on — on a television as well as on this computer.
