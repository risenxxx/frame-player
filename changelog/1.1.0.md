### Improvements and fixes

- **Skip trailer.** A chapter called "Trailer" now gets its own "Skip trailer" button instead of being treated as a preview of the next episode.
- **More recaps recognized.** Chapters titled "Re-cap" or "Рекап" now offer the recap skip button.
