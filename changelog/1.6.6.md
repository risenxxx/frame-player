### Improvements and fixes

- **Dropped frames are always recorded.** The dropped-frame counters in media info now note when they last changed even if the panel was closed at the time.
- **Process stalls.** A new row shows the last time the whole player was briefly stopped and the longest such stop. Read next to the dropped frames, it tells whether a hitch came from video playback or from the computer being busy.
