### Improvements and fixes

- **The player says when Video upscaling is getting ready.** The first time Video upscaling starts on a Windows computer it needs ten seconds or more to get ready, and the picture stands still meanwhile. Instead of looking frozen, the player now dims the picture and shows "Preparing upscaling" with a running clock until the video moves again.
