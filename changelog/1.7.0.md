### New

- **SOCKS5 proxy for torrents.** A new setting sends peer connections and tracker announces through a SOCKS5 proxy, a way to get torrent traffic past a network that blocks it by destination. The DHT and UDP trackers stay outside the proxy.
- **Encrypted torrent connections.** Connection encryption can be set to Off, When possible or Encrypted only. It helps where a provider cuts BitTorrent by recognizing the protocol; "When possible" still talks to peers that cannot encrypt, so it never makes things worse.

### Improvements and fixes

- **Updated torrent engine.** The built-in torrent client moves to a newer version, which is what makes the proxy and encryption settings possible.
- **Settings apply straight away.** Changing the proxy or the encryption mode closes the torrent that is playing and says so, since the new setting only takes effect on a fresh connection.
