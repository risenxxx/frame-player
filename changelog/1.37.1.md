### Improvements and fixes

- **Video upscaling no longer freezes the picture each time the player starts.** On Windows the Video upscaling mode needs ten seconds or more of preparation the first time the picture grows large enough for it to run, and the video stood still meanwhile — typically in the middle of resizing the window or going full screen. The player used to throw that work away when it closed and repeat it in every session; it now keeps it, so the pause happens once on a computer rather than every time.
