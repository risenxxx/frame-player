### New

- **The player is a proper Now Playing app on macOS.** Control Center shows the video's name and progress under Now Playing, with working controls; the keyboard's play/pause key works; and taking AirPods out pauses the video, which the previous release promised and did not deliver — the player had been showing up there as "mpv", with buttons that did nothing.

### Improvements and fixes

- **The keyboard works again right after leaving fullscreen on macOS.** Leaving fullscreen used to take the keyboard away from the player until the picture was clicked once: Space, the arrows and every other key did nothing. The keyboard now stays with the player through the transition.
