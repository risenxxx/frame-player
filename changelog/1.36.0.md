### New

- **Sharper upscaling for low-resolution video.** The Picture menu can enlarge a video with a neural network instead of plain scaling — one tuned for films and series, one for animation — for a single file or for its whole playlist. It works only when the picture is shown larger than its own size, such as a 720p episode in full screen, and the menu says when the current size makes it unnecessary.
