### Improvements and fixes

- **Issue reports hide long folder names completely.** A folder whose name runs to many words, as a downloaded release's often does, could be left partly visible in an issue report. The whole path is now replaced, however long it is.
- **Videos no longer open in a separate "mpv" window on Windows.** Every now and then a video started in a second, bare window of its own while the player's window stayed empty, and this happened more often the longer the computer had been running. The picture now always plays inside the player.
