### Improvements and fixes

- **Peer search on Windows.** On Windows the peer network used for magnet links could stop working a minute or two into a session, leaving magnets unable to open until a restart. It now keeps running.
- **Trackers answer again.** Trackers refused the player's requests, so they never supplied peers. The player now listens on a real port and keeps the tracker link's own parameters, including personal passkeys.
