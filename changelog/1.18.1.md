### Improvements and fixes

- **Move the window from the end screen.** When a video has finished, the window can again be dragged by the area around the replay and next-video buttons. Dragging does not cancel the countdown to the next video.
- **The window returns to its place after fullscreen.** Leaving fullscreen on macOS, especially after entering it with a double click on the video, could move the window to the left edge of the screen. It now comes back exactly where it was.
- **Cleaner switch to and from fullscreen on macOS.** The window now goes black for the length of the fullscreen animation and the picture fades back once it has settled. The video no longer stretches for a moment, and the title bar no longer shows up twice or as a strip across the screen during the switch.
