### New

- **Fit subtitles to the frame rate.** Subtitles made for 25 fps on a 23.976 fps video drift further from the speech the longer you watch, which a delay cannot fix. When a downloaded subtitle's frame rate differs from the video's, the player offers to fit it. The subtitle menu also has a Frame rate section with common conversions (25 → 23.976, 23.976 → 25, 24 → 23.976) for any text subtitle. The choice is remembered for each video.

### Improvements and fixes

- **Interface engine version in settings.** The settings footer now shows the WebView2 version on Windows or the WebKit version on macOS, which helps when the interface draws incorrectly over the video.
