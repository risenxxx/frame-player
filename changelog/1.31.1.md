### Improvements and fixes

- **Fast frame stepping now covers 4K videos and downloaded torrents.** Stepping back frame by frame was still slow on 4K files and on anything opened from the torrent list, because the fast path left both out. It now applies to them too: after a short first wait, each step back takes about as long as a step forward. A torrent qualifies once it has fully downloaded. While you step backward the sound track is switched off, which makes getting started about three times quicker, and it comes back when you press play.
