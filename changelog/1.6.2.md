### Improvements and fixes

- **Removed items stay removed.** Removing a torrent episode or a link from the start screen history no longer brings it back the next time the start screen is shown or the player starts.
- **Clearing history is complete.** "Clear history" now also removes remembered titles, remembered track choices and the list of downloaded subtitles. Deleting a torrent now also removes the saved positions of its episodes.
- **Messages above dialogs.** Confirmation popups, such as the space freed after clearing the torrent cache, now appear above an open dialog instead of dimmed behind it.
- **Catalog button.** The outline of the Catalog button on the start screen now renders cleanly on Windows.
