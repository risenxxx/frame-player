### Improvements and fixes

- **The audio and subtitle menus are easier to read at a glance.** The tracks now come first, and "Add file…" and "Find subtitles…" sit below a divider with their own icons, so they can no longer be mistaken for one more track. Clicking the desktop or another app closes an open menu, the way a system menu closes. The arrow at the edge of a long menu or settings page now scrolls it when clicked.
