### New

- **.torrent files.** A .torrent file can be dropped on the window or picked from the link dialog, now called "Link or torrent". Once opened it is remembered as a magnet link, so it keeps working after the file itself is deleted.
- **Any language, in order of preference.** Preferred audio and subtitle languages are no longer limited to Russian and English: add as many as you like from a searchable list and put them in priority order.
- **Subtitles with familiar audio.** A new setting decides what happens when the audio is already in one of your subtitle languages: show subtitles, show only forced ones, or show none.
- **Subtitle search follows your languages.** The language filter in subtitle search now offers your preferred subtitle languages instead of a fixed Russian and English.

### Improvements and fixes

- **Recent links.** A link can be removed from the recent list without deleting any torrent data behind it.
- **Readable link tooltips.** Links with non-Latin characters are shown as readable text rather than percent-encoded strings.
- **Deleting torrents.** A downloaded torrent can now be deleted from the list after it has been watched, unless it is the one currently playing.
