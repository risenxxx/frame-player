### New

- **Stepping back frame by frame is fast on files that used to crawl.** On videos with keyframes seconds apart, typical of downloads from video sites, each step back used to take a second or more. The player now decodes in software while you step, which alone makes a step back several times faster, and on files where it is still slow it decodes the stretch back to the keyframe once and keeps it in memory, so every step after that is instant. Hardware decoding comes back when you press play, which can take up to a second and a half on those same files. The memory this uses is capped at a quarter of your machine's, and it can be switched off under Settings → Video.
