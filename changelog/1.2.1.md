### Improvements and fixes

- **Large files on DLNA TVs.** Some TVs take many seconds to start a large file, and the player gave up too early with "The TV could not open this file". It now waits while the TV is still getting ready.
- **Taskbar progress while casting.** On Windows the taskbar progress bar now follows the TV's position and pause state instead of freezing when casting starts.
