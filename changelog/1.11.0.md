### Improvements and fixes

- **Finding TVs on more networks.** Chromecast and DLNA discovery relied only on multicast, which some routers do not carry between wired, 5 GHz and 2.4 GHz networks, leaving the TV list empty. Each search now also contacts recently used TVs and then every device on the local network directly. VPN tunnels and virtual-machine networks are skipped.
