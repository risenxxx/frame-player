### New

- **Catalog.** A new "Catalog" button on the start screen lets you search for a film or series, or browse what is trending now. Titles come with posters, descriptions and, for series, a list of seasons.
- **Releases to stream.** Choosing a title lists the torrent releases found for it with quality and seeds, sorted by quality or by seeds; your choice of order is remembered. A release opens like any other torrent.
- **Catalog settings.** The catalog can be turned off in Settings, and both the metadata service and the release search service can point at your own instances. Both services see what you search for, which the settings say plainly.
