### New

- **Music gets a screen of its own.** An audio file now shows its cover — the one inside the file, or the picture beside it in the folder — over a blurred copy of itself, with the title, the artist, the album and the year, and the lines of an `.lrc` lyrics file as they are sung. Opening one song queues the rest of the album in track order, the album plays on from song to song without a pause or a countdown, and dropping another song on the window plays it instead of adding it as a soundtrack. Songs start from the top and stay out of "Continue watching"; audiobooks and podcasts longer than twenty minutes still resume. On a Mac the artist, the album and the cover also appear in Control Center.
- **ReplayGain.** Settings → Audio can level music by the loudness tags written into the files, per track or per album.

### Improvements and fixes

- **Volume amplification can be turned off.** Settings → Audio has a switch for volume above 100%. With it off, the keys and the mouse wheel stop at the file's own level instead of carrying on to 150%.
