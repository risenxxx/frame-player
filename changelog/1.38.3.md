### Improvements and fixes

- **A calmer hover on the "Continue watching" cards.** Pointing at a card no longer draws a ring around its frame: the picture moves a little closer and the progress bar steps forward, with the whole length of the video shown under it. The previous and next cards at the end of a video lose the ring too and move closer the same way.
