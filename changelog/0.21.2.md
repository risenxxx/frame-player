### Improvements and fixes

- **Consistent sizing.** Several dialogs, menus, buttons and popups had edges a few pixels off or content that overflowed its box. They now line up.
- **Tooltips on the start screen.** Tooltips for recent videos now appear below the card instead of covering its poster.
