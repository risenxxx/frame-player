### New

- **Move subtitles with the mouse.** Grab the subtitles and drag them where they read best: the main ones up and down (plain text subtitles; styled ones keep the place their file gives them), the second ones anywhere on the picture, snapping to the middle as they pass it. Both stay where you leave them for every film; put low on the screen, longer second subtitles grow upwards instead of off the edge, and "Move back to the top" in the second subtitles menu returns them. Subtitles you have placed above the controls no longer jump when the controls appear.

### Improvements and fixes

- **Second subtitles use less memory and keep up with a resize.** The second subtitle is now drawn together with the picture instead of over it, so it no longer makes the player hold on to around 200 MB of extra graphics memory at times, and it stays in place while the window is being resized. It looks and sits as before; when the controls appear it now moves out of their way at once rather than sliding.
