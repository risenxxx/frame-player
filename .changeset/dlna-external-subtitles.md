Subtitle files beside the video now reach the television over DLNA

When you cast over DLNA with a subtitle file chosen in the player (an .srt next to the video, one you added or one you downloaded), the television now gets it along with the film. Picking another subtitle file while the television plays sends the film again from the same moment with the new one. Subtitles inside the file were already listed on the television and are still switched there.
