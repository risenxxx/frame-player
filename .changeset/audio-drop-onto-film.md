Music dropped onto a film plays instead of replacing the film's sound

Dropping an audio file onto a playing video always added it as an extra audio track, so a song dropped on a film kept the picture and swapped its soundtrack for the music. Now the player compares lengths: an audio file about as long as the video, or named after it, is still added as a dub, and anything else — a song, an album — is opened and played.
