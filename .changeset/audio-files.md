Music gets a screen of its own

An audio file now shows its cover — the one inside the file, or the picture beside it in the folder — over a blurred copy of itself, with the title, the artist, the album and the year, and the lines of an `.lrc` lyrics file as they are sung. Opening one song queues the rest of the album in track order, the album plays on from song to song without a pause or a countdown, and dropping another song on the window plays it instead of adding it as a soundtrack. Songs start from the top and stay out of "Continue watching"; audiobooks and podcasts longer than twenty minutes still resume. On a Mac the artist, the album and the cover also appear in Control Center.
