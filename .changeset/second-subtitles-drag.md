Move subtitles with the mouse

Grab the subtitles and drag them where they read best: the main ones up and
down, the second ones anywhere on the picture, snapping to the middle as they
pass it. Both stay where you leave them for every film; put low on the screen,
longer second subtitles grow upwards instead of off the edge, and "Move back to
the top" in the second subtitles menu returns them. Subtitles you have placed
above the controls no longer jump when the controls appear.
