Playback speed in round steps, and on the wheel

The speed keys now step through 1.25, 1.5, 1.75, 2 and so on rather than multiplying, which gave 1.56× and 1.95×. The speed is always shown on the control bar: scroll over it to change it, click it for 1×. The wheel with Alt held changes the speed anywhere over the video; the key can be changed or turned off in Settings → Playback.
