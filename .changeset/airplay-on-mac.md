Play on an Apple TV or an AirPlay television from a Mac

The cast menu on macOS has an AirPlay row that opens the system's own list of AirPlay receivers. Pick a television and it plays the file itself while the player becomes its remote: pause, play and seeking work from the window, and disconnecting carries on in the player from the same moment. Files the television cannot take as they are are repacked first, without re-encoding the picture. If nothing starts, the player says so and suggests checking that AirPlay is turned on in the television's settings. AirPlay is not available on Windows.
