The player starts on macOS 15

On macOS versions older than 26 the player opened but could not play anything and showed "Could not start mpv": the libraries it plays video with had been built for macOS 26 only. They now work on macOS 15 and later, and an older macOS says plainly that the app needs a newer system instead of opening a player that cannot play.
