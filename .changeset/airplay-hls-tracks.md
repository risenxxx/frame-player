Subtitles and instant track switching over AirPlay

When a film with several audio tracks or with text subtitles plays on a television over AirPlay, the player's audio and subtitle menus now switch what the television plays at once, without reloading — including subtitle files you loaded yourself and dubs from the release's own folders. Some televisions do not take a picture in 4K or HDR this way; the player then notices, shows the film in its original quality instead, and remembers that for that television. A new gear beside the AirPlay row lets you choose between this automatic behaviour, always tracks and subtitles, and always the original quality, and shows what each television has been found not to take.
