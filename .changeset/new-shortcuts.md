New shortcuts, and a search for them

Speed back to 1× (=), show the time (T), open the settings (Ctrl+,), close the file (Ctrl+W) and quit (Ctrl+Q) are now keys you can reassign like the rest. A search box at the top of the shortcuts list finds an action by name, by group or by the key it is on.
