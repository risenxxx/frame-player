AirPlay plays the dub you chose, including one from the release's folders

When a film with several audio tracks plays over AirPlay, the television now plays the one you picked in the player, including a dub that sits in a separate file in the release's own folders. Switching tracks restarts playback on the television for a few seconds. Subtitles cannot be sent over AirPlay yet: while a film plays on a television that way, the subtitle menu says so instead of offering choices that would change nothing there.
