Two subtitles at once

The subtitle menu has a "Second subtitles" row: pick another track and it is
shown at the top of the picture, in the same look as the first and stepping
down below the title bar while the controls are up — handy for learning a
language. It can come from a file you add or from the subtitle search, has its
own delay, is remembered for the folder like the first one, and a shortcut
(Alt+V, Option+V on a Mac) hides it for a while without forgetting it. The
subtitle button reads both languages, for example RU+EN.
